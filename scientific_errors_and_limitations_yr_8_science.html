The 4-Part Graph Analysis Formula:

1. TREND General Direction

Does the line increase, decrease, fluctuate, or stay constant over time?

2. EVIDENCE Specific Numbers

Quote starting, ending, or peak values with correct units (e.g., from 30 km to 2,890 km).

3. HIGHLIGHTS Dips / Peaks

Mention sudden changes, maximums, minimums, or unusual outliers.

4. ASSUMPTION Underlying Premise

What must be true for this data/trend to hold valid between or beyond points?
